# POS & Accounts Pro v10 — Full Business System Architecture

## Core Goal
Transform OfflinePOS into a complete business management platform.

## Core Engines

1. Inventory Engine
- Products
- Categories
- Stock movements
- Barcode
- Purchase receive
- Sales deduction
- Returns

2. Transaction Engine
All business activities create transactions:
- Sale
- Purchase
- Expense
- Payment
- Return

3. Customer Engine
- Customer profile
- Ledger
- Credit balance
- Payments
- Statements

4. Supplier Engine
- Supplier profile
- Purchase history
- Payables
- Payments

5. Finance Engine
- Cash in
- Cash out
- Daily closing
- Profit calculation
