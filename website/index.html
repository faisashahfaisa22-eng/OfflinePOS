When cloud backup is used, QAMVIO encrypts it on the device before upload. The backup server receives encrypted data and cannot read your plain business records.

QAMVIO POS FAQ

Common questions about QAMVIO POS, inventory and Android business management.

What is QAMVIO POS?

QAMVIO POS is an Android point-of-sale and business management app for sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, accounts, expenses, loans, cash book, reports, backup and restore.

Can QAMVIO POS manage inventory?

Yes. It includes products, purchases, current stock, stock adjustments, stock ledger, reorder information and stock value reporting.

Does QAMVIO POS include customer and supplier accounts?

Yes. Customer and supplier balances, payments, receipts and detailed statements are part of the business workflow.

Which languages are supported?

QAMVIO POS supports English, Pashto, Dari and Urdu.
