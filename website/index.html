Offline POS software for Android

QAMVIO POS is built around an offline-first workflow for day-to-day business operations. Sales, inventory and account records can be managed on the Android device, while backup and restore tools help protect important business data.

The app brings together point of sale, product and stock management, purchase records, customer and supplier accounts, expenses, loans, cash movement, daily closing and business reports.

QAMVIO POS FAQ

Common questions about QAMVIO POS, inventory and Android business management.

What is QAMVIO POS?

QAMVIO POS is a free Android point-of-sale and business management app for sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, accounts, expenses, loans, cash book, reports, backup and restore.

Can QAMVIO POS manage inventory?

Yes. It includes products, purchases, current stock, stock adjustments, stock ledger, reorder information and stock value reporting.

Does QAMVIO POS work without internet?

Yes. Normal local use is offline. Cloud backup is optional and needs internet when you choose to use it.

Which languages are supported?

QAMVIO POS supports English, Pashto, Dari and Urdu.
