Offline POS software for Android

QAMVIO POS is built around an offline-first workflow for day-to-day business operations. Sales, inventory and account records can be managed on the Android device, while backup and restore tools help protect important business data.

The app brings together functions that small and growing businesses commonly need: point of sale, product and stock management, purchase records, customer and supplier accounts, expenses, loans, cash movement, daily closing and business reports.

Inventory management with stock history

Products can be tracked through opening stock, purchases, sales and adjustments. QAMVIO also includes a stock ledger so businesses can review how quantities changed over time. Read more on our inventory management page.

POS records that connect with accounts

Sales are connected to customer or salesman balances, while purchases and supplier transactions contribute to supplier balances. This gives the POS, stock and account records a shared business flow. Learn more about QAMVIO offline POS software.

What is QAMVIO POS?

QAMVIO POS is an Android point-of-sale and business management app for sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, accounts, expenses, loans, cash book, reports, backup and restore.

Yes. Customer and supplier balances, payments, receipts and detailed statements are part of the business workflow.
