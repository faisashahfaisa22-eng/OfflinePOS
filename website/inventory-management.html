Inventory management for Android businesses

QAMVIO POS includes inventory tools for tracking products, purchases, sales, stock adjustments and the resulting current quantity. Stock information is connected with the sales and purchase workflow instead of being maintained as a separate list.

Track how stock changes

Current stock is built from opening quantity, purchases, adjustments and sold quantity. The stock ledger provides a movement history so businesses can review why a product quantity changed.

Products and reorder information

Product records include pricing, cost, opening quantity and reorder information. This helps users identify low-stock items and understand the value held in inventory.

Purchases and supplier records

Purchase entries add stock and can connect with supplier balances. Supplier payments, receipts and statements are available in the same business system.

Inventory reports

QAMVIO includes stock reporting, product sold information and export options for business review and record keeping.
