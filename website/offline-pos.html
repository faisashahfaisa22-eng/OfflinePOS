Offline POS software for Android

QAMVIO POS is designed for businesses that need sales, stock and account records available in one Android app. Its offline-first workflow supports day-to-day operations even when internet access is limited or unreliable.

Point of sale and invoice workflow

Create sales invoices, record received cash, track due or recovery amounts, discounts, vehicle or reference information and invoice history. Sales can connect with customer or salesman balances so the sales record and account record stay part of the same business flow.

More than a simple sales app

QAMVIO combines POS functions with products, purchases, stock ledger, customers, suppliers, expenses, loans, cash book, daily closing and business reports. This reduces the need to keep separate records in multiple tools.

Business records and backup

QAMVIO includes backup and restore tools for protecting business records. The app also includes user access controls and security features intended to protect local business data.

Who can use QAMVIO POS?

The feature set is suited to businesses that need sales, stock and account tracking, including shops, trading businesses and operations that also manage fuel or oil sales.

Languages

QAMVIO POS supports English, Pashto, Dari and Urdu.
