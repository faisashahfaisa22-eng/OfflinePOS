QAMVIO uses a quote-first process. This page does not collect card details or accept an instant payment.

How payment works

  1. Request a POS service quote or request a digital project quote.
  2. Review and accept QAMVIO's written scope, total price, currency, timeline and terms.
  3. QAMVIO sends a unique, secure Stripe-hosted Payment Link to your confirmed email address.
  4. Before paying, verify that the link opens a genuine Stripe-hosted checkout and that the amount, currency and merchant match your accepted quote.
  5. Complete payment on Stripe's secure checkout. QAMVIO confirms the payment in its Stripe Dashboard before beginning work.
