Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how QAMVIO POS handles business information used in the application.

Business data

QAMVIO POS may store business records such as products, sales, customers, suppliers, expenses, loans, statements and reports. Storage and synchronization behavior can depend on the version and features being used.

Security and backups

QAMVIO POS includes access-control, backup and data-protection features. Users should protect passwords, recovery information, devices and backup files.

Third-party services

Some versions may use services for cloud backup, hosting or other application functions. Where third-party services are used, their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

Contact

For QAMVIO support or privacy questions, use the official QAMVIO website or the project support channel on GitHub.
