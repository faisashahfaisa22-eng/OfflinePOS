Terms of Use

Last updated: October 2, 2026

These terms describe the general use of QAMVIO POS as a business-management application.

Use of the app

QAMVIO POS is provided as a tool for managing business records. Users are responsible for reviewing invoices, balances, taxes, reports and other business information before relying on them for financial or legal decisions.

Backups and account security

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate backups and safeguarding login credentials, recovery information and devices.

Availability

Features may change as QAMVIO POS develops. Software and network-dependent features may occasionally be unavailable or require maintenance.

Contact

For support, use the official QAMVIO website or the project support channel on GitHub.
